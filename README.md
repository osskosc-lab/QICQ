# QICQ

QICQ is maintained as a **falsification-first research framework**. Distinct research lines share a common claim firewall but do not automatically raise one another's evidential status.

## Active research lines

### 1. H_QC Phase 1 — Xenon Isotope Replication

The confirmatory biological target is deliberately narrow: independently test whether a xenon-isotope-dependent anesthetic phenotype replicates under a frozen, blinded, blocked design before any stronger quantum-mechanism or consciousness claim is considered.

See:

- `preregistration/H_QC_Phase1_Xenon_Isotope_Replication_v1.1.md`
- `simulations/hqc_phase1_design_audit.py`

A successful H_QC Phase 1 can establish only the preregistered xenon-isotope-dependent phenotype. It cannot by itself establish nuclear-spin causality, a quantum mechanism, or quantum consciousness.

### 2. QICQ QBG Phase 0A — Quantum Behavior Qualification Gate

QBG Phase 0A is a separate **methodological infrastructure line**. It asks whether a candidate bridge quantity can distinguish a generic coherence diagnostic from a stronger quantum resource under a frozen synthetic free-set test.

The current Phase 0A deliberately uses a two-qubit proxy:

- diagnostic: basis-dependent off-diagonal norm (Q_delta);
- free set: separable two-qubit states;
- resource proxy: entanglement negativity;
- stress tests: local-basis invariance, local free-channel monotonicity, and separable/classical mimic exclusion;
- optional operational witness: maximal CHSH value;
- frozen qualification: 100 seeds beginning at 20260909.

See:

- `qbg/phase0a/QICQ_QBG_Phase0A_v0.1.md`
- `qbg/phase0a/manifest.json`
- `qbg/phase0a/qbg_phase0a.py`
- `qbg/phase0a/test_qbg_phase0a.py`

**Critical firewall:** Phase 0A is not a process-matrix causal-nonseparability test. A Phase 0A pass does not establish indefinite causal order, quantum-controlled causal order, a Quantum Switch, a biological quantum mechanism, or consciousness.

## Repository policy

This repository separates:

1. frozen hypotheses, free sets, thresholds, and stopping rules;
2. computational qualification / falsification audits;
3. later real-world or process-level execution;
4. interpretation and claim ceilings.

Synthetic qualification is evidence about **gate mechanics only** unless a protocol explicitly states otherwise.

## Next methodological boundary

A future QBG Phase 0B may begin only after Phase 0A core gates pass. Phase 0B must replace the density-operator proxy with a genuine process representation and pre-freeze process validity constraints, the causally separable free set, a certified CNS/QCO quantity or witness, adversarial fixed-order+coherence mimics, operational access assumptions, and free-supermap monotonicity scope before execution.

No synthetic result in this repository is evidence for a biological or quantum-consciousness mechanism.
